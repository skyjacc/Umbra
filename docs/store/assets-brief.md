# Store assets — Umbra EQ 2.5.0

The six store cards use captures of the actual native Chrome popup from the production 2.5.0 ZIP. The extension was opened in a separate Chrome instance and operated through computer use (CUA). Each 620 × 580 popup capture is placed in a 1280 × 800 composition with explanatory text. This asset work does not change extension code or redesign the interface: the original left-hand master control and bottom navigation are retained.

## Three screenshots per locale

1. **Tune the tab you are listening to / Настройте звук текущей вкладки.** Show the actual EQ with a moderate preset, readable graph and a genuinely captured tab. The primary image should make the purpose clear at thumbnail size.
2. **A sound for each website / Свои настройки для каждого сайта.** Show actual site rules and their selected presets. Explain that a saved rule selects the sound after capture starts; do not depict automatic capture of every new tab.
3. **Start with a preset, make it yours / Выберите пресет и настройте под себя.** Show the four existing built-ins and a real user preset. Include the precise band controls where they remain legible. Do not show proposed Night or headphone AutoEQ controls.

For a short demonstration video, show one real flow: play an audio sample, start capture, apply a preset, change its curve, compare with Bypass, then stop. Explain the browser's capture indicator. Use owned or appropriately licensed demo audio. Matched playback level helps viewers distinguish tone changes from a simple loudness increase.

## Technical requirements

- Supply 1–5 screenshots at 1280 × 800 (preferred) or 640 × 400. Use JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha, as required by the dashboard.
- Capture an interface composed for that canvas; do not stretch a 620 × 580 popup into the screenshot aspect ratio.
- Use square corners and full-bleed images without an outer padding frame. Keep explanatory text brief and the actual product readable.
- Small promotional tile: 440 × 280. Marquee: 1400 × 560. These are separate brand assets, not additional screenshot slots.
- Localized screenshots and videos may be supplied for EN and RU; small and marquee tiles are global assets.

See [Supplying Images](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/images), [Creating a great listing](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/best-listing), and [Localized listings](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/cws-dashboard-listing).

For future updates, verify the three saved image previews and their order in both languages. Keep a dated record of uploads and distinguish a saved draft from a submitted or published update.
